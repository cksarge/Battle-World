# Privacy Policy

**Effective November 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Battle World (the "Game"), made and run by Carter K ("we", "us"), collects, how it's used and what choices you have. It applies to the website at battleworld.carterscoding.com and everything in the Game. By using the Game you agree to this policy and to the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md).

## 1. The short version

- We collect what the Game needs to run accounts, online matches, rankings and moderation.
- We don't sell your information, show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
- Guests who never sign in keep their data in their own browser; it isn't sent to us unless they play online features that need it.

## 2. What we collect

**When you create an account**

- Your email address and username.
- Your password, which is stored hashed by our account provider. We never see it.
- When you agreed to these documents and when your account was created.

**When you play while signed in**

- Stats and progress: matches, wins, losses, kills, deaths, headshots, ratings, XP, levels, season progress, challenges, achievements, weapon mastery and match history.
- Your operator and loadout setup, cosmetic items you own and items given or removed by an admin.
- Social features: friends and friend requests, party and clan membership, your online status and what you're playing (shown to friends), and commends.
- Custom maps you upload and maps you like.
- Text chat in lobbies and matches. Chat is sent live to the other players. It is not saved by us, except when a player reports someone: the reported player's chat from that match is attached to the report.
- Reports, ban appeals and feedback you send. Feedback can include your game version, browser (user agent), screen size and graphics settings if you leave that option ticked.
- Moderation and anti-cheat records: warnings, mutes, bans, friendly-fire penalties and automatic flags when a match sees something that looks like cheating (for example impossible speed or fire rate).
- Anti-cheat checks on your own game: during online matches, your game checks its own page for signs of cheat tools. If it finds any, it sends a flag with what it found: the names of built-in browser functions that were replaced, the web addresses of scripts added to the game page (which can identify a browser extension or userscript), keyboard or mouse input made by a script, or the timing of your clicks and reactions when they look automated. Nothing is sent when nothing is found.
- Match replays for reviews: when your game flags another player for possible cheating, or you report a player from a match's results, your game sends us its replay of that match (everyone's positions, actions and in-game events, plus the usernames of the players in it). Only moderators use these, to check what happened.
- Your vote in each poll you vote in.
- If you're a moderator: messages you send to and receive from the admin in the admin panel, which can mention other players, and the moderation actions you take.
- Sign-in attempts by username and time, used to lock out password guessing. These are deleted after a day.

**About your device**

- A random device ID created by the Game and kept in your browser. When you sign in, it's linked to your account so bans can't be dodged with new accounts. It isn't a fingerprint and doesn't contain anything about you or your device.
- Like any website, the services that deliver the Game (see section 4) receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser connects to them.

**Kept only in your browser**

Settings, keybinds, guest stats and progress, saved replays (apart from the review replays above), map drafts, the offline-play copy of the Game (if you turn it on) and small notes like whether you've seen the tutorial are stored in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. They stay on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

## 3. How we use it

- To run the Game: sign you in, save your progress, match you with other players, show leaderboards and profiles, and deliver chat, parties, friends and clans.
- To keep the Game fair and safe: review reports and anti-cheat flags, enforce the Terms of Service and handle appeals.
- To fix bugs and improve the Game, using feedback and the information attached to it.
- To send emails you need, such as account confirmation and password reset. We don't send marketing emails.

## 4. Who it's shared with

- **Moderators**: the admin can let trusted players help moderate. Depending on what they're allowed to do, moderators can see reports, anti-cheat flags, review replays, match records, moderation history, accounts that have played on the same device, and who voted for what in polls, and they can message the admin about players. Only the admin sees email addresses.
- **Other players** see your username, operator, cosmetics, level, rank, rating, stats, achievements, clan and the maps you upload. Friends can see when you're online and what you're playing. Chat goes to the players in your lobby or match.
- **Service providers** that run the Game for us:
  - Supabase (database, accounts, emails and realtime multiplayer, hosted in the United States).
  - GitHub Pages (hosts the website) and GitHub's public API (the latest-version link in the menu).
  - jsDelivr (delivers the 3D and database code libraries).
  - Cloudflare Turnstile, when turned on, to stop bots at sign-in and sign-up.

  These providers process information under their own privacy policies.
- **The law**: we may share information if the law requires it, or to protect players, the Game or others from harm or fraud.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep account information for as long as your account exists. Moderation records (reports, bans, flags and device IDs linked to bans) may be kept after an account is deleted, to stop banned players from coming back. Sign-in attempt records are deleted after one day, and review replays after 60 days. Information kept only in your browser lasts until you clear it.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- You can play as a guest without an account.
- You can untick the option that attaches device details to feedback.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or have your account deleted, through the in-game Feedback button. We may need to confirm the account is yours first.
- Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local privacy laws. Contact us and we'll help.

## 7. Children

The Game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable protections such as encrypted connections, hashed passwords and database access rules. No system is perfectly secure, though, so we can't guarantee your information will never be accessed without permission.

## 9. Where your information is processed

The Game and its providers are based in the United States. If you play from elsewhere, your information is sent to and processed in the United States.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and we'll tell you in the Game when it changes in a meaningful way. Continuing to use the Game after that means you accept the updated policy.

## 11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy can be sent through the Feedback button in the Game's menu, or as an issue on the Game's GitHub repository.
